About

CA Daily turns Press Information Bureau (PIB) releases into a daily digest for banking and government exam aspirants. Releases are ranked by exam relevance (RBI/finance first), categorised, and linked back to the original on pib.gov.in.

Disclaimer

Summaries are extracted automatically and categories are keyword-based, so they can be wrong. Government press releases are not exam-curated: verify facts at the source. This site is not affiliated with PIB or the Government of India.

Privacy

No accounts or tracking. Saved items, read marks, quiz streaks and theme are stored in your browser only.
